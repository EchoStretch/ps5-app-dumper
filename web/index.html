Dumps

Rename all dumps - the game dumps listed below get the title id alone, PPSA01325-app0, or the game's title behind it, PPSA01325-app0_ASTRO's_PLAYROOM. DLC keep their names. A dump ShadowMount runs a game from is left as it is, since the link names its folder.
ShadowMount is running. It links every dump it finds to the installed game of the same id - the game then starts from the dump (MOUNT) and cannot be dumped again, and a dump you move within its reach is linked anew. Stop it to keep what you move unlinked; start it again whenever you like, from wherever you usually do.
